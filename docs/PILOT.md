# Pilot plan (a plan: none of this has happened yet)

**Goal:** find out whether students understand and remember a topic better with Eklavya.

**Who:** one batch at one college, 20 to 30 students, one subject they have an exam in.

**When:** two weeks, before their internal exam.

**What students do**
1. Upload the subject's syllabus or their notes.
2. Use Eklavya for at least 3 short sessions a week.
3. Answer the three-question card in the app.

**What we measure**
- From the app: how many students came back in week 2, voice minutes, flashcards rated, mastery change per topic.
- From the feedback card: found it easier than the textbook, trusted the citations, rating out of 5.
- Before and after: a 10-question test on two topics, one studied with Eklavya and one without.

**Cost:** about $5.50 per student per month at 20 minutes, 3 times a week, and under about $14 at the daily limit (Google's published Gemini prices, checked 3 November 2026).

**Success looks like:** more than half still using it in week 2, and a higher after-test score on the Eklavya topic.
